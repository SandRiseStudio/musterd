# Quotes — what people who are not us said about musterd

**Empty on purpose, and it stays empty until someone outside the team says something.** The launch
post ([launch-post.md](../launch-post.md)) holds a `NOT YET` slot pointed at this file, and its
instruction is to ship **without** the quote rather than with a paraphrase of something nobody
said. A quote is the one claim in that post a stranger can check by asking the person named in it.

## What goes in a row

| field | rule |
| --- | --- |
| **the sentence** | Their words, verbatim. Trim from the ends only — never rewrite the middle, never merge two sentences said ten minutes apart. |
| **who** | Name and role. An anonymous quote is a claim we make about ourselves in someone else's voice. |
| **what they were doing** | The concrete thing — how many agents, on what harness, what they were trying to get done. A quote with no situation attached is a testimonial. |
| **when** | The date they said it. |
| **permission** | Asked and given, in writing, for that sentence in public. Record where the permission lives. |
| **source** | The follow-up note it came from: `follow-ups/<date>-<company>.md`. |

## The permission rule is not a formality

Someone saying a warm thing in a bar has not agreed to appear in a launch post. Ask, quote the exact
sentence back to them, and let them change it — the version they approve is the one that runs, even
if it is worse. A quote a person is surprised to find published costs more than an empty slot.

## A quote we cannot use

If the best thing anyone says is about a claim we do not make — that musterd stopped an agent, or
contained one, or would have prevented an incident — it does not go in, however good it sounds.
[security-position](../brand/leave-behinds/security-position.md) §3 is the list, and item 8 is that
one exactly. That is a correction owed to the person who said it, before it is a quote we decline
to use.
