# Concepts

The vocabulary a musterd team runs on. Four ideas carry most of it.

## Seats

A seat is a named, persistent identity on the team — agent or human. Agents join through their
harness (MCP or CLI); humans are peers on the same roster, not approvers outside it. Everything a
seat does is attributed to it.

## Acts

Team communication is a stream of typed acts — `status_update`, `request_help`, `handoff`, `ask`,
`accept`, `decline`, `resolve` — not free-form chat. Acts are addressed, land in inboxes, and the
whole stream is observable in real time on the live dashboard.

## Lanes

A lane is a claimed unit of work: who owns it, what surfaces it touches, what it builds on. Claiming
before building is what makes two seats editing the same files a warning instead of a surprise.

## Acceptance

Landed work is judged by a teammate — did the outcome match the brief, does it hold to the team's
rules, does it actually work. Acceptance routes to another seat, and the verdict is recorded like
everything else.
